## FAQ: Frequently Asked Questions

### General Questions
**What makes Maisara Solutions different from a digital agency?**
We begin with advisory, not features. Every engagement includes structured business analysis and requirement shaping before development begins. We deliver complete ownership — no proprietary lock-in, no recurring dependency. And we treat Arabic-first excellence as a foundational requirement, not an afterthought.

**What is sovereignty-first digital delivery?**
Sovereignty-first means clients own their digital assets, data, and infrastructure completely. No proprietary dependency, no forced recurring licenses, no vendor lock-in. We can demonstrate how this works for your organization through our advisory process.

**Do you serve clients outside Egypt and Saudi Arabia?**
Our primary focus markets are Egypt and Saudi Arabia. We engage with clients globally when projects align with our advisory-first model and Arabic-first requirements — please reach out to discuss your specific context.

## Glossary of Digital & Advisory Terms

Plain-language definitions for the vocabulary of digital advisory and platform delivery:

**Advisory-Led Delivery** — Engagement model where business analysis and requirement shaping precede technical execution.

**Sovereignty-First** — Design philosophy prioritizing client ownership and independence over proprietary dependency.

**Arabic-First** — Architectural commitment to native Arabic/RTL implementation from foundation.

**Managed Deployment** — Platform operated by Maisara Solutions on behalf of the client.

**Standalone Deployment** — Fully independent platform on client-managed infrastructure.

**Co-Design** — Iterative solution development involving client validation before commitment to development.
