# Legal Center & Documentation

*Public-facing repository for Maisara Solutions legal agreements, terms, policies, and compliance documentation — transparent, accessible, and reviewed regularly.*

---

## Purpose

The Legal Center provides all legal documentation governing the Maisara Solutions client relationship in one place. We believe transparency is a form of trust — clients and partners should be able to review terms easily, understand their rights and obligations clearly, and access documentation without navigating bureaucratic barriers.

All documents are presented in plain language where possible, with Arabic versions available upon request.
