# Referral & Growth Program

*Structured pathway for satisfied clients, partners, and ecosystem allies to refer aligned organizations and receive recognition for successful introductions.*

---

## Program Overview

The Maisara Solutions Referral & Growth Program is designed for clients, partners, and allies who believe in our advisory-first, sovereignty-first, Arabic-first model — and want to share it with organizations they believe would benefit.

Successful referrals receive structured recognition, co-branding support, and community acknowledgment. We believe that the strongest growth signal is a trusted introduction from someone who has experienced the value firsthand.

---

## Program Eligibility

### Who Can Refer
- **Existing Clients** — Organizations or individuals who have completed a Maisara Solutions engagement
- **Ecosystem Partners** — Innovation hubs, incubators, co-working networks, and professional service providers
- **Specialist Network** — Trusted technologists and advisors in our specialist network
- **Community Members** — Active participants in our community forums and events

### Eligibility Requirements
- Understanding of Maisara Solutions' differentiation focus and target segments
- Ability to make warm introductions to aligned organizations
- Commitment to honest and transparent referral communication
- Acceptance of referral program terms and guidelines

---

## Referral Submission Portal

### How to Submit
1. Identify organization you believe would benefit from Maisara Solutions
2. Gather contact information and context for the introduction
3. Submit through the referral portal with:
   - Referred organization name and contact
   - Your relationship to the contact
   - Brief note on why this is a good fit
   - Preferred timing for introduction

### Submission Categories
- **Direct Referral** — Warm introduction you will facilitate
- **Ecosystem Introduction** — Connection through formal partnership or hub relationship
- **Content-Inspired** — Organization reached through educational content or social sharing

## Referral Success Stories

### What We Share
We document referral success stories with:
- Referrer permission and consent
- Anonymized or attributed presentation as agreed
- Focus on organizational outcomes, not referral mechanics
- Educational value for prospective clients and partners

### What We Don't Share
- Financial or contractual details of referral arrangements
- Referrer commercial expectations or compensation structures
- Any confidential referral relationship terms

## Referral Program Terms & Guidelines

### Core Terms
- Referrals made in good faith with honest communication about Maisara Solutions' differentiation
- No misrepresentation of capabilities, pricing, or engagement models
- Introduction facilitated transparently between parties
- Referral recognition processed fairly and consistently

### Prohibited Practices
- Misrepresentation of Maisara Solutions' positioning or capability
- Financial incentives or compensation arrangements outside recognized program terms
- referrals made under false pretenses or conflicts of interest
