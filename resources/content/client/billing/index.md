*Complete billing transparency, subscription management, and cost visibility for Maisara Solutions managed-service clients.*

## Subscription Plan Overview

Managed-service engagements operate on clear, documented subscription models:

### Plan Types

**Managed Operations — Standard**
- Monthly managed infrastructure, monitoring, and operational support
- Comprehensive backup and security management
- Advisory availability within defined cadence
- Platform updates and performance optimization

**Managed Operations — Enhanced**
- Full standard managed operations
- Enhanced advisory availability including content strategy and feature evolution
- Priority incident response
- Biannual strategic review sessions

**Advisory Retainer**
- For standalone clients or organizations seeking advisory without managed operations
- Scheduled advisory sessions and availability
- Digital strategy and feature evolution guidance
- No infrastructure management

### Plan Modification
- Plan upgrades and downgrades available with 30-day notice
- Change effective at next billing cycle
- Pro-rated adjustments where applicable

## Payment Failure & Recovery Support

### Failure Protocol
If payment fails:
- Immediate notification to client
- Grace period with continued service access
- Direct advisory support for resolution
- Service continuity during resolution window

### Recovery Process
- Alternate payment method coordination
- Billing inquiry and dispute resolution
- Service continuity during resolution
- Confirmation of payment processing and updated status

## Subscription Cancellation & Offboarding

### Cancellation Process
1. Written cancellation request with notice period
2. Advisory confirmation meeting to discuss transfer or wind-down
3. Platform transition planning (standalone migration or handover)
4. Final invoice and payment settlement
5. Asset delivery and documentation handover

### Offboarding Commitment
- Complete platform and data handover within agreed timeline
- Transfer or migration support where possible
- Final asset delivery and documentation
- Relationship closure with appropriate documentation

[Cancel Subscription](/client/billing/cancellation)

*We prefer continued partnership to cancellation. If you are considering cancellation, we invite a conversation about whether a plan modification might better serve your needs.*
