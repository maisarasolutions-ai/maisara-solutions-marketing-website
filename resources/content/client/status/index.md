# System Status & Platform Transparency

*Real-time and historical data on the Maisara Solutions platform's health, performance, and incident history — operational transparency as a trust mechanism.*

---

## Real-Time Status Dashboard

### Current Status
The System Status Dashboard provides real-time visibility into:
- **Platform Availability** — Overall system operational status at a glance
- **Managed Environment Health** — Infrastructure health for managed deployments
- **Core Services** — Authentication, content management, analytics, and advisory platform status
- **Regional Performance** — Egypt and Saudi Arabia regional performance indicators
- **Incident History** — Recent incidents, current status, and resolution timelines

### Status Indicators
- **Operational** — All systems functioning within parameters
- **Degraded Performance** — Partial service impact with active mitigation
- **Partial Outage** — Specific service impact with defined resolution plan
- **Major Outage** — Broad service impact with emergency response
- **Maintenance** — Scheduled maintenance with advance notification

## Incident History & Post-Mortem Archive

### Incident Documentation
Every significant platform incident is documented with:
- **Incident Overview** — Date, time, affected services, and impact scope
- **Root Cause** — Technical identification and explanation
- **Impact Duration** — Time to detection, response, and resolution
- **Affected Users** — Scope of impact and client notification
- **Resolution** — Remediation actions and preventive measures
- **Post-Incident Actions** — Follow-up improvements and monitoring enhancements

### Post-Mortem Philosophy
We publish post-mortems to:
- Demonstrate transparency with clients and prospects
- Maintain accountability for platform reliability
- Share learnings that inform organizational improvement
- Build trust through honest communication about platform operations

[Browse Incident History](/client/status/incidents)
