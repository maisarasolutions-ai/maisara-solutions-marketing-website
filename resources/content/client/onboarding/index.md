# Client Onboarding Portal

*The guided onboarding experience for new Maisara Solutions clients — from engagement confirmation through platform access, training, and first advisory touchpoints.*

---

## Welcome Sequence

Congratulations on your decision to partner with Maisara Solutions. This onboarding portal is your structured introduction to the engagement — designed to reduce uncertainty, accelerate time-to-value, and ensure you feel supported from the moment your engagement is confirmed.

### What to Expect
The onboarding journey typically spans 2–4 weeks, depending on engagement complexity and deployment model. Every phase is facilitated, documented, and reviewed — ensuring clarity and confidence.

---

## Engagement Confirmation

### What You Receive
Upon engagement confirmation, you receive:
- Signed engagement agreement and terms confirmation
- Project brief and advisory scope documentation
- Advisory team introduction and primary contact designation
- Onboarding timeline and milestone calendar
- Platform access credentials and initial setup information

### What We Need From You
- Designated points of contact for decisions and approvals
- Administrative access to your domain and hosting (if applicable)
- Content assets and brand guidelines for platform customization
- Stakeholder calendar availability for discovery and validation sessions

---

## Platform Access & Credential Provision

### Managed Deployment
- Platform environment provisioning within defined SLA
- Admin credential delivery with security guidance
- Initial access testing and validation
- Mobile and desktop access configuration support

### Standalone Deployment
- Infrastructure environment coordination
- Hosting and domain access arrangement
- Technology partner introductions (if applicable)
- Technical credential and access documentation

---

## Advisory Kickoff Preparation

### Discovery Session Preparation
Before your first structured advisory conversation:
- Review organizational objectives documented in engagement agreement
- Identify key stakeholders for discovery interviews or workshops
- Prepare examples of platforms, experiences, or content you admire
- Document technical and operational constraints or preferences

### Kickoff Agenda
Typical kickoff covers:
- Project scope review and confirmation
- Stakeholder roles and decision-making frameworks
- Advisory cadence and communication protocols
- Success criteria and measurement approaches
- Risk identification and mitigation planning

---

## Project Timeline & Milestone Orientation

### Typical Engagement Timeline
1. **Week 1-2:** Discovery, scoping, and architecture confirmation
2. **Week 3-6:** Design, development, and iterative validation
3. **Week 7-8:** Arabic/RTL quality validation, UAT, and launch preparation
4. **Week 9:** Launch, handover, and initial training

### Milestone Checkpoints
- Design validation and approval
- Content and workflow confirmation
- Arabic/RTL quality review
- User acceptance testing and feedback incorporation
- Go-live preparation and contingency planning

---

## Client Orientation & Platform Training

### Training Delivery
Tailored to organizational size and technical capacity:
- **Solo Professionals:** 1:1 platform administration walkthrough
- **Small Teams:** Group training with role-specific access and workflows
- **Organizations:** Multi-session program for admin, content, and management layers

### Training Modules
- Platform navigation and content management
- Bilingual publishing workflows and Arabic/RTL guidance
- User administration and role management
- Analytics and operational dashboard access
- Advisory and support escalation paths

---

## Team Collaboration Setup

### Admin and Contributor Access
- Role-based access configuration
- Multi-user platform administration
- Content publishing permissions and workflow
- Approval and publishing sequences where needed

### Communication Channels
- Designated advisory contact and escalation path
- Client success platform or communication tool setup
- Emergency and time-sensitive support protocol
- Regular advisory cadence scheduling

---

## Communication & Support Protocol

### Advisory Cadence
- Weekly check-ins during active development
- Bi-weekly or monthly advisory sessions post-launch
- On-demand advisory availability for urgent decisions

### Support Channels
- Designated communication platform (email, messaging, or platform tooling)
- Response time commitments and escalation paths
- Emergency support protocol for platform-impacting issues

---

## Document & Asset Delivery Checklist

At engagement completion, you receive:
- Complete platform source code and documentation
- Admin training materials and platform walkthrough documentation
- Asset library: logos, templates, and brand deliverables
- Technical architecture documentation for standalone environments
- Operational handover and advisory transition plan

---

## Post-Launch Advisory Scheduling

After launch, we transition from delivery to ongoing advisory:
- First 30-day post-launch check-in
- Bi-weekly advisory sessions for first 90 days
- Monthly advisory sessions thereafter (adjustable based on engagement)
- Scheduled strategic review sessions every quarter

## Feedback & Onboarding Experience Loop

### Your Feedback Matters
We collect structured feedback at key onboarding milestones:
- Design validation and approval

Your feedback directly shapes our onboarding process, platform templates, advisory approach, and support quality. We treat onboarding as a living practice, not a fixed procedure.
