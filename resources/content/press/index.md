## Crisis Communication Protocol

In the unlikely event of a platform incident or operational issue requiring public communication:

- **Acknowledgment** — Timely, accurate acknowledgment of the situation
- **Client Communication** — Direct notification to affected managed clients
- **Public Communication** — Appropriate public statement aligned with legal counsel
- **Resolution Communication** — Clear explanation of remediation and prevention measures
- **Follow-Up** — Continuous update until resolution is confirmed

Crisis communication is handled directly by the founder with legal counsel engagement where necessary.
