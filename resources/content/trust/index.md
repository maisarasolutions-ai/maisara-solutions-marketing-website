## Data Residency & Sovereignty

### Standalone Deployments
Complete client control over data location, hosting infrastructure, and backup architecture. You determine where data resides and how it is protected.

### Managed Deployments
Strict logical isolation ensures your data remains separate from all other clients on the managed platform. Data residency is aligned with regional infrastructure capabilities and your regional compliance requirements.

**Core Commitment:** Your data belongs to you. Full access, full portability, full transparency.

## Security Posture & Architecture Overview

Maisara Solutions operates at enterprise-grade security standards through:

- **Infrastructure Security** — Automated patching, firewall management, and access control
- **Application Security** — Standards-based authentication, input validation, and secure coding practices
- **Network Security** — Isolated client environments in managed deployments; no cross-tenant data access
- **Monitoring & Detection** — Continuous security monitoring with structured alerting and incident response
- **Security Testing** — Regular validation of platform security and isolation boundaries
- **Vulnerability Management** — Structured patch management and security update cadence

Security is designed into architecture, not bolted on after delivery.

## Backup & Disaster Recovery Transparency

Every Maisara Solutions platform — standalone and managed — is protected by automated backup architecture:

- **Automated Daily Backups** — Point-in-time snapshots of all client data and platform configurations
- **Retention Policies** — Configurable retention aligned with client requirements
- **Recovery Testing** — Periodic validation of backup integrity and recovery procedures
- **Geographic Separation** — Backup isolation from primary infrastructure for resilience
- **Client Access** — Backup inquiry and recovery coordination through advisory channel

Backups are not invisible promises. They are tested, documented, and auditable protections.
