## Two Implementation Paths

Every Maisara Solutions platform can be delivered through one of two implementation models. Both deliver the same features, the same Arabic-first quality, and the same sovereignty guarantees. They differ only in operational responsibility.

### Standalone Deployment
Fully independent platform on dedicated infrastructure you control. Ideal for organizations with technical capacity or long-term sovereignty requirements.

- Complete infrastructure control
- Your hosting and domain decisions
- Full operational independence
- Higher initial investment, lower recurring dependency

[Migrate path from Managed to Standalone available](/deployment-models/migration)

### Managed Deployment
Branded environment on the Maisara Solutions network. We operate infrastructure while you focus on content and growth. Ideal for organizations prioritizing speed, simplicity, and managed predictability.

- Rapid time-to-value
- Managed security, updates, and backup
- Predictable operational costs
- Lower initial investment
- Migration to Standalone available as you grow

[Compare Side-by-Side](/deployment-models/comparison)
