## Confidentiality & Privacy

We take client confidentiality seriously. Some engagements cannot be publicized due to industry sensitivities or contractual requirements. We maintain:

- Client consent before any portfolio documentation
- Anonymized or generalized presentation where appropriate
- Technical architecture discussion without exposing IP or implementation details
- Clear separation between public portfolio and confidential client work

Our portfolio is a curated reflection of engagements where clients have agreed to share — and where sharing demonstrates capability effectively.
